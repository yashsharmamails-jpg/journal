## 5. Mistakes

1. **No 1m confirmation before entry.** The trading model requires MSS or bearish displacement on 1m before entering. Price was bouncing bullishly off the 4,402 low when entry was taken — no bearish structure shift had occurred on the execution TF.
2. **FOMO-driven timing.** Entered because the FVG level was "there" rather than waiting for price to show rejection. Seeing the level is not the same as getting confirmation.
3. **Selling into momentum.** The 1m chart showed a +19.5 pt rally off lows. Selling into bullish momentum without a shift is counter-trend on the execution TF.

## 7. Improvement Suggestions

1. **Wait for 1m MSS before entry.** The FVG is the zone — not the trigger. The trigger must come from 1m structure (MSS, CHoCH, or bearish displacement candle closing below prior swing low).
2. **Define "rejection" visually.** A bounce INTO the FVG is not rejection. Rejection = price enters FVG, stalls, then displaces away with bearish momentum.
3. **Add a pre-entry checklist rule:** "Is the 1m making lower lows + lower highs? If no → do not enter."
4. **Screenshot HTF before entry.** Capture the 15m/1D thesis with markup BEFORE clicking sell — forces deliberate planning.
