## Lessons from specific trades

### 20260527-XAUUSD-01
- **Lesson:** Having the right level means nothing without the right timing. The 15m FVG was valid. The entry was not — because 1m was bullish (bouncing off lows). The model says: zone + 1m confirmation = entry. Zone alone ≠ entry.
- **Rule:** "If the 1m is making higher highs and higher lows, I do not sell — regardless of how good the HTF zone looks."

### 20260527-XAUUSD-02
- **Lesson:** Better entry than Trade #1 (at-zone, not chasing). Trade went into profit — then reversed to SL. The missing piece was BE management. If SL had been moved to BE when price was 1R in profit, this would have been breakeven, not a loss.
- **Rule:** "When price moves 1R in my favor (or reaches first liquidity target), move SL to breakeven. Protect capital first."
- **Second lesson:** Feeling "very confident" immediately after a loss is a yellow flag. Confidence should come from process adherence, not from emotional need to prove yourself right.
- **Rule:** "After a loss, my next entry requires A++ criteria — not just A. The bar goes UP, not down."
